THE PROBLEM → THE SOLUTION

Parking is broken. We turn existing cameras into the fix.

Drivers waste 20–30 minutes searching for spots. In Almaty alone — 600K+ vehicles — there's no way to know where a free space actually is.

40–60% off-peak vacancy, no occupancy analytics, no dynamic pricing, no digital channel. Revenue sits empty in plain sight.
